# SmartGate AI · 90-second demo

## Problem · 15 seconds

Apartment security needs to distinguish a familiar vehicle from an authorized visit. A plate lookup alone cannot show whether a vehicle actually entered, whether a visitor was approved, or whether it later exited.

## Demonstrate · 45 seconds

Open Live Simulator. Say: "This is our software gate and fictional resident community. I’ll show a complete visit."

Press **Play full entry + exit demo**. Narrate: "The vehicle is checked against the registry. The barrier opens, the car crosses, and the barrier closes. The entry creates a visit. On exit, the barrier opens and closes again, and the same visit is completed. Every step is logged."

Select **Unknown vehicle**, then **Start entry**. Say: "An unknown plate never opens the gate automatically." Choose the destination resident and approve or reject. Say: "Resident identity and consent are simulated for this prototype. Approval grants this visit; it does not register the vehicle."

Show occupancy and Access Logs. Filter ENTRY / EXIT and download the audit.

"SmartGate links perception to a clear authorization decision and an auditable vehicle visit. We keep permission, passage and departure separate. The gate and resident actions are simulated; our registry, policies and audit trail are real software."
